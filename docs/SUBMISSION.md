## Questions to rehearse

Why no vector database? The bounded case is small enough to provide all scoped
records; avoids retrieval omissions. Larger corpora need evaluation-led retrieval.

Does a quote prove a conclusion? No. It proves provenance, not interpretation.
The separate reviewer and human decision layer address, but do not eliminate, that gap.

Why two commitment assessments? A target can be observed missed while its
prerequisite was not met. That is different from declaring unconditional breach.

Can the reviewer share the same blind spots? Yes, especially using the same
model. Its output is advisory and never silently overwrites the report.

What is missing for production? Authentication, persistence, connector access
controls, data retention policy, account-based quotas and broader live evaluations.
